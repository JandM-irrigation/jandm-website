Turning your sprinklers on after winter isn't just flipping a switch. Freezes, snowplows and settling ground can damage heads and lines over the winter. A professional start-up catches problems before they waste water or kill your lawn.

What's included

  • Slowly turn the water back on to protect the pipes
  • Check the backflow device
  • Run every zone and look for leaks or broken heads
  • Adjust heads for full coverage
  • Program your controller for spring
When to book

Usually April or May, after the last hard freeze.
