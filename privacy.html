Privacy Policy

How J&M Irrigation Services handles the information you share with us.

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you request a quote, we collect what you enter: your name, phone number, email (if given), city, the service you need, your preferred language and any message.

How we use it

Only to respond to your request, schedule work and follow up about your service. We do not sell or rent your information.

How it's stored

Requests are delivered through our website host's form service and by email, and may be copied to our customer management system. We keep them only as long as we need them.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site, such as which pages are viewed and how people find us. It uses cookies and collects information like your general location, device and browser, but it doesn't tell us who you are. You can opt out with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Cookies and other services

Apart from Google Analytics, this site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, some photos load from Pexels and the reviews on our home page come from Google. These services may log basic technical data such as your IP address.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, email jandm.irrigation24@gmail.com or call 303-549-0712.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version on this page with a new "last updated" date. See also our Terms & Conditions.
