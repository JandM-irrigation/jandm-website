Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you request a quote, we collect what you enter: your name and phone number, the service you need and, on the contact page, your email, city, preferred language and message if you give them.

How we use it

Only to respond to your request, schedule work and follow up about your service. We do not sell or rent your information.

How it's stored

Requests are delivered through our website host's form service and by email, and may be copied to our customer management system. We keep them only as long as we need them.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site, such as which pages are viewed, how people find us and whether a quote request was sent or a phone link was tapped. It uses cookies and collects information like your general location, device and browser. We never send your name, phone number, email or message to Google Analytics. You can opt out with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Cookies and other services

Apart from Google Analytics, this site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The review excerpts on our home page are copied from our Google reviews. These services may log basic technical data such as your IP address.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, email jandm.irrigation24@gmail.com or call 303-549-0712.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new "last updated" date. See also our Terms & Conditions.
