Sprinkler Blowouts

Sprinkler Blowouts in Denver, CO

Protect your sprinkler system from cracked pipes and costly spring repairs.

Get a Free QuoteCall 303-549-0712

Water left in your sprinkler lines expands when it freezes. On the Front Range, a single cold night can crack pipes, valves and backflow devices. A blowout clears the water out with compressed air so your system makes it through winter safely.

What's included

  • Shut off water to the system
  • Clear each zone with compressed air
  • Drain the backflow device
  • Set your controller to off for the winter

When to book

Most Front Range homeowners book in October, before the first hard freeze. Spots fill fast once cold weather is in the forecast.

New install customers

If J&M installed your system, your first blowout is free.

Questions we hear a lot

Can I skip a year?

It's a gamble. One hard freeze can cause repairs that cost much more than a blowout.

Do I need to be home?

It's best if you are. That way we can reach the shutoff and backflow, and walk you through anything we find.
