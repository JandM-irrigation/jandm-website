Questions we hear a lot

Do you charge for estimates?

Estimates for new sprinkler systems are free. Repair visits start with a $75 diagnostic, and that fee is waived if you have us do the repair.

Is there a warranty?

Every new sprinkler system comes with a 1-year warranty, and your first fall blowout is free.

When should I book my blowout?

Most Denver-area homeowners book in October, before the first hard freeze. Spots fill fast once cold weather is in the forecast.

Can you fix a system another company installed?

Yes. We work on any irrigation system, no matter who installed it.

What areas do you serve?

Denver, Aurora, Commerce City, Thornton, Parker, Castle Rock and Larkspur, plus larger projects in Colorado Springs.
