Complete new irrigation systems for new landscapes, larger lots and acreage, planned for your soil, slopes and Colorado's weather.

About this service

A sprinkler system is only as good as its install. On a large property a poor layout means dry spots, wasted water and years of repairs, so J&M plans every zone around the land before trenching starts.

Projects J&M takes on

  • New systems for new landscapes and new builds
  • Larger lots and acreage
  • Full replacements of old or failing systems
  • New sod installed with the system

What to have ready for an estimate

The property's location and rough size, the areas that need water, whether there is an old system, and whether new sod is planned. J&M can install the system and lay the sod together, so the new lawn is watered from day one.

How the work goes

  1. Walkthrough and estimate

    We look at the yard with you and give you an estimate.

  2. Zone plan

    Zones are laid out for sun, shade and slopes, and we explain the components and controller before work begins.

  3. Installation

    Trenching, pipe, valves and sprinkler heads.

  4. Controller setup

    The controller is set up and programmed for your zones.

  5. Final test

    Every zone is run and checked before we leave.

Questions we hear a lot

Do you install systems on larger properties and acreage?

Yes. Larger installations are J&M's focus, especially in Castle Rock, Douglas County and Colorado Springs. Share the location and rough size in your request so we can plan the walkthrough.

Can you install sod at the same time?

Yes. Doing both together saves time and makes sure the new lawn is watered from day one.
