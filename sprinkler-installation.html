About this service

A sprinkler system is only as good as its install. A poor layout means dry spots, wasted water and repairs you should not need, so we plan every zone around your yard.

What to have ready for a quote

  • Your address and the areas of the yard that need water
  • Whether there is an old system on the property
  • Whether you are also planning new sod

Coordinating with new sod

Planning sod at the same time? We can install the system and lay the sod together, so the new lawn is watered from day one.

How the work goes

  1. Walkthrough and estimate

    We look at the yard with you and give you an estimate.

  2. Zone plan

    Zones are laid out for sun, shade and slopes, and we explain the components and controller before work begins.

  3. Installation

    Trenching, pipe, valves and sprinkler heads.

  4. Controller setup

    The controller is set up and programmed for your zones.

  5. Final test

    Every zone is run and checked before we leave.

Questions we hear a lot

Can you install sod at the same time?

Yes. Doing both together saves time and makes sure the new lawn is watered from day one.
